# Day 3 - Bivariate Exploratory Data Analysis

## Objective

The objective of Day 3 was to identify the relationship between customer attributes and churn using statistical summaries and visualizations.

---

## Analysis Performed

- Churn Distribution
- Gender vs Churn
- Contract Type vs Churn
- Payment Method vs Churn
- Age vs Churn
- Tenure vs Churn
- Monthly Charges vs Churn
- Total Charges vs Churn

---

## Key Business Findings

### Contract Type
Customers with Month-to-month contracts exhibit the highest churn rate, while customers with Two-year contracts demonstrate the strongest retention.

### Payment Method
Electronic Check users have a noticeably higher churn rate compared to customers using other payment methods.

### Tenure
Customers with shorter tenure are significantly more likely to churn than long-term customers.

### Monthly Charges
Higher monthly charges are associated with increased customer churn.

### Age
Age shows little relationship with customer churn and is unlikely to be a strong predictive feature.

### Gender
Gender has minimal impact on customer churn behaviour.

### Total Charges
Customers with higher accumulated charges generally remain with the company longer, reflecting greater customer lifetime value.

---

## Business Conclusion

Customer churn is primarily driven by behavioural and service-related factors rather than demographic characteristics. Contract type, tenure, payment method, and monthly charges will be prioritized during feature engineering and machine learning model development.
